03Approach

A clear process. Short lines.

Four steps, every time. You always know where we are and what comes next.

01

Explore

We start with the problem, not the technology. What do your users need, and what is the smallest version that already delivers value?

02

Design

Sketches become clickable screens. You see early how the product will feel and can adjust before anything is built.

03

Build

In short rounds, with AI as an accelerator and a second check on every step. You follow along with a test version on your own phone.

04

We handle publication in the stores, measure what happens and keep improving the product.

05Questions

Good to know.

Is your question not listed? Feel free to ask it using the form below.

What kind of projects do you take on?

Apps for iOS and Android, web apps and digital tools for businesses, clubs and entrepreneurs with a good idea. From a first working version to a complete product with accounts, payments and notifications.

What does it cost to have an app built?

That depends on what you need. After a first conversation you receive a clear proposal with a defined scope and price, so you know where you stand in advance.

How long does it take?

A first working version is often ready within a few weeks. After that we build on in short rounds, so there is always something for you to try.

How do you use AI?

AI helps us prototype, test and review code faster. The decisions remain human work: every change is reviewed before it goes live. In your product we only use AI where it genuinely adds value.

What happens after launch?

That is when it really starts. We keep the product secure and up to date, follow what users do and release improvements when they are needed.
